ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Intelligent Irrigation for Tomato Farming

The Smart Irrigation Advisory System is an AI-assisted agricultural solution designed specifically to support tomato farmers in making better irrigation decisions.

The system combines real-time sensor information with AI-based predictions to provide useful irrigation recommendations.

Our Goal

To support efficient water usage while maintaining suitable irrigation conditions for tomato cultivation.
